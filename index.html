FIELD NOTES / 03 · IBDP PHYSICS IA GUIDE

How to design a high-scoring Euler’s Disk IA.

Why it works: A wobbling coin combines rotational mechanics, energy dissipation, and acoustics with a dramatic power-law singularity—giving rich quantitative data from simple lab equipment.

Recommended Experimental Method (Smartphone Spectrogram): Record the spinning coin’s sound at 48 kHz using Phyphox or Audacity. The sudden final clap marks tft_ftf​ to within ±2 ms, and the fundamental frequency ridge gives f(t)f(t)f(t) at dozens of time points without needing a 1,000 fps camera.

Strong Independent Variables (choose one): (1) Disk radius RRR using machined brass/steel disks of equal thickness, (2) Surface hardness/compliance (glass vs. acrylic vs. wood), or (3) Ambient air pressure in a bell jar to test Moffatt’s air-viscosity hypothesis.
